# CineCraft Connect: user guide

For people who use the app on the web or on their phone. It explains what each part is for and what the buttons do. If you want to check that everything works, see [TEST_PLAN.md](TEST_PLAN.md). If you want to know how it is built, see [APP_ARCHITECTURE.md](APP_ARCHITECTURE.md).

Some things differ between the web and the phone. Where they do, this guide says so.

## 1. Getting started

### What CineCraft Connect is
A place for people in film and entertainment to show their work, meet and hire each other, run projects together, pitch stories to producers, rent gear and talk.

### Choose an account type
| Account | Best for | You can |
|---|---|---|
| **Fan** | Following the industry | Follow creators, join discussions, rate films, read announcements and company pages. You cannot post, apply for jobs, pitch or use the marketplace. |
| **Pro (Creator)** | Working professionals | Everything: post, connect, message, join and run projects, apply for and post jobs, pitch, use the marketplace and vendors, set your availability. |
| **Studio** | Production companies | Everything a Pro can do, with a Studio badge. Studios can post pitch calls and company pages. |

### Sign up and set up your profile
1. Open the app and choose **Sign up** (or **Login** if you already have an account). Use your email and a password. The eye icon in the password box shows or hides what you type.
2. Complete your profile when asked: name, username, your craft (for example Director, Cinematographer), your location, a short bio and a photo.
3. You can switch between accounts you have saved on this device from **My Profile → Switch Account** or **Settings → Account**.

### Finding your way around
- **Web, wide screens:** a column of icons on the left. Move your mouse over it and it opens to show the names. The items are Home, Search, ProjectSpace, Discussions, Jobs, Network, Marketplace, Pitch, Ratings, Announcements, Pages, Messages, Notifications, Create and your Profile. **More** at the bottom holds Settings and Log out (and staff tools for staff). Fans see a shorter list.
- **Web, narrow windows:** a top bar and a row of tabs at the bottom.
- **Phone:** tabs along the bottom (Home, Discussions, Ratings, Announcements, Pages, ProjectSpace, Jobs, Network) with a **More** tab for the rest.
- **Back button:** pages have a **Back** button at the top. Settings pages have one too.

## 2. Home feed

The feed shows posts, mixed with small sections ("widgets") of things worth a look.

### Tabs and filters (top of the feed)
- **For You:** posts we think you will like.
- **Network** (for Pros and Studios) or **Following** (for Fans): posts from people you are connected to or follow, pages you follow, and your own posts. If it is empty, a **Grow your network** (or **Find creators to follow**) button takes you to the Network page.
- **Craft chips** (Directing, Cinematography, Screenwriting, Acting, VFX): only posts from people in that craft. Tap again to clear.
- **Customize** (button at the end of the tabs): switch the sections between posts on or off. You can also tap **Hide this section** under any section.

### New posts
When other people post while you are reading, a pill appears: **New post available** or **3 new posts**. Tap it to load them.

### Reading
- Scroll down and more posts load by themselves.
- **You're all caught up** appears above the first post you had already seen.
- Pictures are shown in full width and cut to at most a 4:5 shape. A post with several pictures has arrows, a counter (for example 1/3) and dots. You can also swipe.
- Double-tap a picture to like it.

### Under every post
- **Like** (heart), **Comment**, **Share**, **Save** (bookmark).
- **Comments are turned off for this post** appears when the author switched comments off.
- Polls: choose an option to vote. You can change your vote. Results show after you vote.

### The ⋮ menu on a post
For other people's posts:
- **Copy link** (web only).
- **Why am I seeing this?** Shows the reason, for example "You follow Sam" or "Because you liked posts about #cinema".
- **Not interested:** the post never comes back. Undo it from **Settings → Blocked accounts → Hidden posts**.
- **Mute #tag:** posts with that topic stop appearing. Manage it in **Settings → Blocked accounts → Muted words**.
- **Mute [name]:** their posts leave your feed. They are not told. Undo it in **Settings → Blocked accounts → Muted accounts**.
- **Block [name]:** you stop seeing each other's posts, and they cannot message you.
- **Report Content:** tell the moderators about something that breaks the rules.

For your own posts:
- **View insights:** people reached, views, likes, comments, shares, saves.
- **Edit Post** and **Delete Post**.
- On the phone you can also **Pin to Profile**.

### Sections between posts
Announcements, People to connect with, Trending projects, Active discussions, Popular in marketplace, Trending vendors and New releases. Each has a **View** or **See all** link. On the web, a card has an **X** to dismiss it for two weeks. In **Connect with Creators**, the X remembers that you are not interested in that person.

### Right-hand panel (web, wide screens)
Your name and **Switch** (change account), **Suggestions for you** with **Connect** and a dismiss X, **Your Companies**, and **Suggested pages**.

## 3. Creating a post

Open **Create** (the + in the navigation, or the box at the top of the feed: "Share your latest project or idea…").
1. **Pick pictures or videos** (up to the limit shown), or write a text post.
2. **Edit:** crop and aspect ratio, filters, text on the picture, adjustments. **Next** to continue, **Discard** to throw it away.
3. **Details:**
   - Caption (write or add an emoji), tags and mentions.
   - **Add a GIF** (search, then choose).
   - **Location:** type a city and pick it.
   - **Tag people** and **credit collaborators** (they get a request to accept; up to five).
   - **Who can see this post:** everyone, connections only, or only me.
   - **Post category.**
   - **Turn off comments** and **Hide like counts**.
   - **Add a poll:** a question, two to four options, and how long it stays open (one day, three, seven or no end).
   - **Schedule for later:** choose a date and time; until then only you can see it, and you can publish it now or change the time. People get notified when it goes live.
   - **Link preview:** a card for the first link in your text; **Remove link preview** if you do not want it.
   - Mark the post as AI-generated if it is.
4. **Post.** A draft is saved automatically (**Saved draft**).

## 4. Your profile and other people's profiles

### Your profile
- **Header:** photo, name (with the verified tick if you are verified), @username, craft and location, your bio and social links. On the right: your Posts, Followers and Connections counts (tap Followers or Connections to see the list) and your buttons.
- **Buttons (bottom of the right column):**
  - **Pencil** (Edit profile).
  - **Switch accounts.**
  - **Share profile.**
  - **Availability calendar.**
  - **Settings.**
- **At a glance** (Pros and Studios): your work availability, day rate, the cities you work in and memberships, if you have set them.
- **Profile is X% complete:** a list of things to add (experience, a portfolio piece, a confirmed credit, a showreel, and so on). Tap one to go and do it.
- **Highlights:** showreel, languages and gear.
- **Tabs:** Posts, Portfolio, Projects, Announcements, Analytics, Saved, Credits, Skills, Experience, Awards, Recommendations. You can hide some sections from your public profile.

### Edit profile
Photo, name, username, craft (type to search or add one), bio, location, website and social links (Instagram, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Facebook, YouTube), who can contact you for direct pitches, and (Pros and Studios) **Availability**: Open to work, Booked (with a date) or Not looking, your day rate range, cities, a note and who can see it (Everyone or Connections only). **Save Changes** saves everything at once.

### Someone else's profile
Buttons in the right column:
- **Connect** (sends a request, optionally with a note) → becomes **Request Sent** → **Connected**. Fans **Follow** instead.
- **Ask for introduction:** ask a mutual connection to introduce you.
- **Message:** appears when you are connected, or when the person's privacy allows messages from you.
- **Direct Pitch:** send a story privately (only if you write stories, they take direct pitches, and they are a producer or studio).
- **Work together:**
  - **Request dates:** ask to hold their dates for a role. **Needs a connection** (a lock shows until you are connected).
  - **Invite to project:** invite them to one of your project spaces. **Needs a connection.**
  - **Shortlist:** add them to one of your private crew shortlists.
  - **Recommend** (connections only): write a recommendation they can approve.
  - **Availability:** open their availability calendar.
- Top bar: **Back**, **Share**, **Report**, **Block**.

## 5. Network

Find and keep in touch with people. Tabs:
- **Connections:** people you are connected to. Search, sort, filter by tag. On each card: **Message**, and the ⋮ menu with **View Profile**, **Notes & tags** (private notes and labels for you), **Remove Connection**, **Report**, **Block**.
- **Requests:** requests you received (**Accept** / **Reject**) and sent (**Withdraw**).
- **Discover** (and **Discover Creators** / **Discover Fans**): suggestions with the reason (for example "3 mutual connections"). **Connect** or **Follow**, or the X to remove a suggestion (you can see them again under **Ignored**). Filters: craft, skills, gear, language, city, **Available now**.
- **Crew shortlists:** your private lists of people for a project.

## 6. Messages and calls

- **Messages** shows your conversations. **Add** starts a new one (search users). You can search inside messages.
- Send text, attachments and emoji. Chats can be end-to-end encrypted: your phone or browser creates a key; keep your backup safe (see Settings → Security).
- Start a voice or video call from a chat. Rooms and project spaces have their own calls.
- **Delete Conversation** removes it for you.
- You can only message people whose privacy settings allow it. Blocked people cannot message you.

## 7. Projects and project spaces

- **ProjectSpace** lists projects: **All**, **My Projects**, **Bookmarked**. Each card has **Share**, **Report**, and (yours) **Edit** and **Delete**, plus **View open roles**.
- **Create Space** makes a new project: name, description, genre, location, roles you need, budget, dates and a picture.
- A project's page shows the team and open roles and has **Enter space**.
- The **space** is your team's workspace: chat, files, tasks, schedule, budget and call sheet, role invitations and a call. Only members can enter.

## 8. Jobs

### Finding work
- **Jobs** lists openings. Search and filter, **Sort** (Newest First, Highest Salary, Lowest Salary), **Clear All Filters**.
- On a card: the bookmark to save, **Apply Now**, **Applied** once you have.
- **Alert me about jobs like this** creates a job alert.
- Open a job to see details, then **Apply**, **Save**, **Share**, **Report**, **Withdraw** (your application) or **Visit Page Profile** (the company).
- **My Applications:** your applications and their stage. When an employer invites you to an interview you can **Confirm**, **Decline** or **Can't make it**, and **Add to calendar**. **Withdraw Application** cancels one.

### Hiring
- **Post a new job** (title, description, requirements, pay, location, screening questions).
- **Manage Postings:** each job can be **Published** or closed, **Duplicate**d, **Deleted** (drafts), **Copy link**, **View posting**.
- Views: **Pipeline** (drag people through stages with **Move to…**), **List**, **Analytics**.
- Tools: search, **Shortlisted** filter, **Shortlist** or **Remove shortlist** for many people at once, **CSV** export.

## 9. Pitch

### For writers
- **Discover Pitch Calls:** briefs posted by producers. Filter by genre, language, format. **Save** one, then **Submit Your Pitch**.
- **Submit a pitch** in three steps: **Your Story** (title, logline, synopsis, genre, language, tone), **Story World** (character notes, outline, links) and **Final Step** (credentials, up to three **file attachments** (PDF or images, 15 MB each), NDA signature if the call needs one, rights and originality declarations). A draft is kept on your device. Press **Submit**.
- **Direct Pitch:** from a producer's profile. It creates a private brief and sends your pitch there.
- **My Pitches:** your submissions and their status. You can **Edit** a pitch until the producer starts to review it, **Withdraw** it, open **NDA record** (print or save as PDF), read the **Message from the producer**, and **Upload Full Deck (PDF)** when one is requested.
- You see a call as **Guild (claimed)** when the producer entered a guild number. It is not verified.

### For producers and studios (call owners)
- **Post a Pitch Call** (title, description, format, genre, language, budget, compensation, deadline, NDA required, open to debut writers). Your own call has **Edit This Call**, **Close call** / **Re-open call**, **Duplicate** and numbers (views, saves, pitches).
- **Review Inbox:** pitches sent to you. Filter by status. **Select** several pitches to **Compare (2-3)** side by side or apply **Under review / Shortlist / Pass** to all at once. **Export as spreadsheet** (web).
- Open a pitch to read it, sign the NDA if the call needs one, open the writer's files, set a status (Seen, Under review, Shortlist, Interested, Request full deck, Invite to discuss, Pass, Start Collaboration), keep a **private note and 1 to 5 stars**, send a **message to the writer**, **Report this pitch**, **Block this writer** or open the **NDA record**.
- Calls close by themselves after their deadline. People who saved a call and have not pitched are reminded the day before it closes.

### Story Exchange
Writers list a concept (logline, teaser, genre, stage, asking deal, NDA required). Producers press **Request Access** (and sign the NDA if needed). The writer sees **Incoming Access Requests** and presses **Approve** or **Decline**. After approval the producer sees **Full Synopsis Unlocked** and can **Open Chat with Writer**. The locked text is never sent to anyone who is not approved.

## 10. Marketplace and vendors

- **Marketplace** has tabs: All Listings, Equipment, Locations, Vendor Services.
- Open a listing: pictures, price, availability, reviews. **Contact seller / Book**, **Share**, **Report**, **Leave Review**; owners see **Edit** and **Delete**.
- **My Bookings:** **Renting** (things you rent) and **Hosting** (people renting yours). Actions: **Accept**, **Decline**, **Mark completed**, **Cancel booking**, **Leave review**, **Message**.
- **Wishlist** ("My Gear Wishlist"): saved equipment and bundles, **Share** your list with a link, grid or list view.
- **Vendors:** service providers. A vendor page has **Request a quote**, **Chat with Vendor**, **Review**, **Report**, **Share**, and for owners **Add Package**, **Get verified**.
- **My Quotes:** **My requests** and **Received**. Actions: **Send quote**, **Accept quote**, **Decline**, **Mark completed**, **Cancel request**, **Review vendor**, **Message**.
- **Seller Hub:** how your listings and vendor businesses are doing, bookings and quotes, switch listings on or off, **Get verified**.

## 11. Discussions

- **Discussion Rooms:** public rooms and rooms you must ask to join. Filter by category, sort, **Clear Filters**.
- On a room: **Request to Join** (then **Request Pending**), **Enter Chat**, **Share**, **Report**; owners get **Edit Room** and **Delete**.
- **Create Room:** name, description, category, tags, who can join.
- Inside a room: chat, replies, pictures, and room calls.

## 12. Ratings and announcements

- **Ratings:** browse films and series (from TMDB), search, open a title. Rate with stars, write a review (hide spoilers), mark reviews **Helpful**, **Report review**, **Add to list**, **Share**. **My Ratings** shows your ratings and lists (**Create** a list, for example "My Top 10").
- **Announcements:** news and calls from the platform and companies. Search and filter; unread ones are counted.
- **Pages (company pages):** company profiles with a follow button. Owners see **Edit**, **Settings**, **Get this page verified**, **Delete**; anyone can **Share** or **Report this page**.

## 13. Search

**Search** finds people, projects, discussions, posts, jobs, pitch calls, announcements, vendors, marketplace listings and companies. Pick a **category**, **Sort**, filter (craft, **Verified only**, listing type) and use **Load more**. Recent and saved searches show when the box is empty. If one category fails, a note says "Some results could not be loaded" and the others still show; use **Try again**.

## 14. Notifications

Open **Notifications** from the bell. They tell you about connection requests, messages, likes and comments, mentions, project and job updates, pitch changes (shortlisted, full deck requested, invited to discuss, passed, closed call), date requests and invitations, and more. Tap one to go to it. The red number is how many are unread.

## 15. Settings

**Settings** has: Account (switch type, link another account, delete account), Privacy (who sees your profile details, contact details, show email and location, blocked accounts), Notifications (push and email, what you get notified about), Messages, Quiet hours, Requests, Calls, Language, Appearance (theme), Accessibility, Sound, Security (password, sessions), Data (download your information), Help (articles and support requests), Legal and About.

**Settings → Blocked accounts** holds four lists: **Blocked accounts** (Unblock), **Muted accounts** (Unmute), **Muted words** (add words or hashtags, remove with ×) and **Hidden posts** (Show again).

## 16. Staying safe

- **Block** someone from their profile, a post or a card. They cannot message you or see your posts.
- **Mute** hides someone's posts without telling them.
- **Report** anything that breaks the rules (a post, comment, profile, listing, pitch call or pitch). A moderator reviews it.
- Contact details are private unless you choose to show them.
- Never share your password. Use **Settings → Security** to see and end other sessions.

## 17. If something goes wrong

| Problem | Try |
|---|---|
| "Some results could not be loaded" in Search | Press **Try again**. If it keeps happening, tell support which category it names. |
| The feed looks old | Pull down (phone) or reload (web). Tap **New post available**. |
| A button is greyed with a lock | It needs a connection with that person. Connect first. |
| You cannot post, apply or pitch | Check your account type. Fan accounts cannot. |
| You cannot message someone | Their privacy settings may not allow it, or one of you blocked the other. |
| A pitch cannot be edited | The producer has started to review it. You can still withdraw it. |
| You need help | **Settings → Help**, then **New request** to contact support. |
